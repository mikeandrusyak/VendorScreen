Privacy Policy — VendorScreen

Effective date: 2026-07-03  ·  Last updated: 2026-08-14

This Privacy Policy explains how the VendorScreen application (“Application”), operated by Andrusiak Mykhailo, an independent individual developer (“we”, “us”), processes data when installed on a monday.com account. The Application is a business-to-business tool. In most cases the Customer (the organization installing the Application) is the data controller and we act as a data processor on the Customer's behalf.

1. Data We Process

We do not intentionally collect special-category (sensitive) personal data. The vendor name you submit may, depending on your data, relate to an identifiable individual — you are responsible for the lawfulness of submitting it (see Section 6).

Note on the audit record: the screening audit trail is stored persistently in our database (see Sections 3–5) so you can export it on demand. It never contains the raw provider response — only the trimmed summary described above. The audit trail is disabled entirely on deployments where no database is configured, in which case screening inputs are processed transiently and nothing is persisted beyond your own monday.com board.

2. How We Use Data

  • To perform the requested sanctions/PEP screening of the vendor name.
  • To write the resulting risk indicator and notes back to your monday.com board.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and maintain the Application.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for any purpose other than the screening you request.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing (IMPORTANT)

To perform screening, the Application transmits the vendor name — and, if you have mapped a country column, the vendor's country — to OpenSanctions (https://api.opensanctions.org), a third-party sanctions and PEP data provider. This transfer is necessary to deliver the Service. OpenSanctions processes the query under its own terms and privacy policy. No monday.com item content other than the vendor name and, where mapped, the country is sent to OpenSanctions.

The Application runs within monday.com's serverless environment (monday code); board updates are sent to the monday.com API. The persistent audit record and usage-metering data (Section 1) are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Neon, https://neon.com), which acts as a sub-processor hosting that data on our behalf. Error diagnostics may be sent to Sentry (https://sentry.io) for reliability monitoring; Sentry is configured not to receive personal data (send_default_pii=False). We do not transfer your data to any other third party except as required by law.

The complete list of third-party services the Application touches is therefore: monday.com (platform + API), OpenSanctions (screening queries), Neon (database hosting), and Sentry (error monitoring, no PII).

4. Data Retention

  • The screening audit record and usage-metering data (Section 1) are retained in our database for as long as the Application remains installed on your account, so that you can export your audit trail at any time.
  • On uninstall, we permanently delete all of your account's data — the full audit trail, usage counters, and account record — within 10 days of receiving monday.com's uninstall notification. In practice the deletion is triggered automatically and immediately by monday's uninstall webhook.
  • Where the Application is deployed without a database, screening inputs are processed transiently and are not stored in any persistent database by the Application.
  • Results are written back to your monday.com board and are thereafter under your control and retention policy.
  • Operational logs may be retained for a limited period for security and debugging, then discarded.
  • You may request earlier deletion at any time via the contact in Section 11.

5. Data Security

  • Communication with monday.com, OpenSanctions, and our database occurs over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Persisted data (the audit record and usage-metering data) is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database that provides encryption at rest; all connections to it are encrypted in transit.
  • Webhook requests are authenticated using monday.com's signing secret / client secret (JWT verification); requests that fail verification are rejected.
  • The Application uses short-lived, per-account session tokens for board updates in production and does not persist any monday.com access tokens.
  • Data is logically segregated per monday.com account — every stored row is keyed by account ID and every read and export is scoped to the requesting account.
  • Secrets are stored as environment variables within the monday code environment and are not committed to source control.

6. Lawful Basis and Customer Obligations

Where the Customer is the data controller, the Customer is responsible for establishing a lawful basis (e.g. legitimate interest or legal obligation for AML/KYC) for processing vendor data through the Application, and for providing any required notices to data subjects. By using the Application, the Customer instructs us to process the submitted data solely to provide the Service.

7. International Transfers

OpenSanctions and monday.com may process data outside your country. Where applicable, such transfers rely on the safeguards offered by those providers. You are responsible for assessing the adequacy of these transfers for your own compliance requirements.

8. Data Subject Rights

Because we act as a processor and do not maintain an independent store of screening inputs, requests to access, correct, or delete personal data should be directed to the Customer as controller. We will assist the Customer in responding to such requests as required by applicable law.

9. Children's Data

The Application is a B2B tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date and, where required, through the marketplace listing.
